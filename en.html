How it was built

French DSCG methods (UE212 corporate finance, UE116, UE121): fundamental diagnosis, multi-model valuation with verified formulas (108/108), CAPM/Jensen, portfolio diversification, EVA/MVA/TSR. Every figure is traceable to a primary source (universal registration documents, press releases); the simulator recomputes everything from monthly data (Yahoo Finance) and the OAT yield (FRED).
